# Folio

Author: Faefolk333

Short description:

A quiet, customizable e-reader for New Nintendo 3DS.

Full description:

Turn your New Nintendo 3DS into a pocket reading companion. Folio puts your book
on the top screen and a minimal touch interface on the bottom. Read your own
TXT, HTML and DRM-free EPUB books from the SD card, with the books you opened
most recently at the front of your library.

- Five reading themes: Light, Warm, Paper, Dark and Red Night.
- Six font choices, adjustable text size, line spacing and letter spacing.
- Optional Bionic Reading emphasis and a Focus Line to guide your eyes.
- Per-book saved reading positions and a bookmark you can set or remove.
- Chapter navigation and page jumps.
- Page turning with L/R or ZL/ZR and line scrolling with the Circle Pad.
- Touchscreen library filters and a selectable books folder.
- Optional lower-screen sleep with touch/SELECT wake.
- English by default, with a complete Czech interface available in Settings.

Current release: 0.4.8, with .3dsx for Homebrew Launcher and .cia for installation
on a CFW-enabled New Nintendo 3DS. The owner confirmed launch and immediate
HOME-menu closing in 0.4.8 on a real console.
Designed for New Nintendo 3DS; other models have not been verified.

EPUB support focuses on reflowable text. DRM, embedded cover artwork, complex
CSS/page layouts and PDF reading are not supported. The library is nonrecursive
and displays up to 256 entries. Font/style preferences are session-only;
language and the books folder persist. Saved progress restores when you open
the book; startup currently opens Library. Loading large books can take time.
Use START to save and exit; wait for a pending settings save before doing so.
